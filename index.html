One-pedal driving

Hold when stationary

⚠ Limits: Fardriver regen cannot bring the bike to a full stop — regen fades to zero near 0 rpm, so one-pedal still needs the brake to stop. "Hold" is anti-rollback (limits roll to a crawl at reduced current, times out after 5 s via ParkTime) — not a true electronic handbrake; use the mechanical brake/side-stand on hills. Regen on a full pack can trip the BMS charge cut-off (losing regen suddenly, or killing power on some BMSs); holding at standstill pushes current through one phase and heats the motor/controller.
